Light Show is a puzzle game built on real Outside Plant (OSP) fiber-optic
engineering. You're a field tech routing light from the OLT to the
customer's ONT — every splice, connector, and splitter you place is modeled
on real-world dB loss figures. Land the receive power inside the target
window, or the customer's link goes down.

FEATURES
- Real link-budget math: fusion vs. mechanical splices, UPC vs. APC
  connectors, PON splitters, wavelength-dependent attenuation — all
  pulled from real OSP practice.
- Outage events: fiber cuts, storm damage, water intrusion, and
  macrobends force a live reroute under a countdown — diagnose and fix
  the link before the customer-complaint timer runs out, then see your
  result on a dedicated pass/fail debrief screen.
- Pick your companion before you start: Séraphine (Fiber), Ondine
  (Coax), Linka (Mobile), or Lattice (Ethernet) — each one is tied to a
  real access technology, has her own dialogue, and reacts in her own
  voice to every splice, outage, and level result.
- An original 8-bit-inspired chiptune soundtrack scores the menu,
  splicing, and outage states, with its own win/fail jingles.
- 64x64 pixel-art visuals with an anime-inspired look, including
  on-board icons for every component you place.
- No ads. No in-app purchases. No tracking. No network permission at all
  — this game works completely offline and always will.

This first release ships Splice School (your first fusion splice) and a
Storm Season outage drill, with more worlds — connector types, PON
split budgeting, and long-haul wavelength puzzles — in active
development for future updates.

Whether you're a working telecom tech, a network engineering student, or
just enjoy a good logic puzzle, Light Show teaches real fiber-optic
concepts through play.
