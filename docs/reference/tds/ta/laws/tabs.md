* For Double Sided Printing, a tab placed on page 89 of WAC lands on the 45th physical sheet.
* If your tab labels extend off the right edge, you'll want to place them on right-hand (odd-numbered) pages — pages 37, 39, 42, 43, 45, 33, 35 in RCW are all odd so they land on the front face of a sheet, which is ideal.

* WAC pages 89, 99, 114, 127, 129 are all odd — good for tabs. Pages 78, 91, 116 are even (back side) — consider tabbing the page immediately before them so the tab is visible from the front.

* WAC-3 (page 89, Telco 09 scope) is your single most important tab — it lists exactly what the Telecommunications specialty can and cannot install, including the underground raceway 50-foot limit, the 10-foot metal raceway limit, and all the excluded systems (fire alarm, intrusion, access control, etc.).
